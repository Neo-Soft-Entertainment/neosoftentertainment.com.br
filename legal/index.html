1. Data Collected and Purposes

Depending on the specific application or service, NEO SOFT ENTERTAINMENT collects only the data strictly necessary for its operation, which may include the following categories:

  • Social Authentication Data: When logging in through a third-party account (such as Google), an application may collect the user's public profile photo exclusively for visual identification and account personalization.
  • Registration Data: Depending on the application, NEO SOFT ENTERTAINMENT may collect the user's full name, CPF, e-mail address, and phone number.
  • Purpose: To ensure legal security, identify users, prevent fraud such as fake profiles, and enable communication between users when required by the application.
  • Geographic Location Data: Some applications may request permission to access the user's real-time location only while the application is in use.
  • Purpose: To enable location-based features, such as displaying content relevant to the user's region or completing address information automatically.
  • Photo Gallery Access: Some applications may request permission to access the user's device photo gallery.
  • Purpose: To allow the user to upload photos for use within the application (such as listings, in-game content, or other user-generated content) or to update their profile picture.

2. Legal Bases for Processing

NEO SOFT ENTERTAINMENT processes user data based on the legal bases established by Article 7 of the LGPD, including:

  • Performance of Contract or Preliminary Procedures: Processing required to fulfill the Terms of Use and provide the features of each application or service.
  • Legitimate Interest: Processing required to maintain the internal security of the ecosystem, improve features based on usage and location, and enhance the user experience.
  • Fraud Prevention and User Safety: Use of identification data, such as CPF when applicable, to validate the identity of users.

3. Data Storage and Security

The security of user data is a priority. NEO SOFT ENTERTAINMENT adopts technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data, including:

  • Encryption in Transit: All information transmitted between the application and servers uses secure encrypted communication protocols, such as HTTPS/TLS.
  • Cloud Storage: Personal data collected by NEO SOFT ENTERTAINMENT's applications and services is stored in secure cloud infrastructure.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: NEO SOFT ENTERTAINMENT does not sell users' personal data.
  • No Advertising Sharing: NEO SOFT ENTERTAINMENT does not share users' personal data with advertising companies.
  • Limited Visibility of Contact Data: A user's contact data may become visible to other users only when necessary to allow interaction or negotiation within a specific application or service.

Unless otherwise stated for a specific application, NEO SOFT ENTERTAINMENT's applications and services are intended for users aged 16 years or older.

Users under 18 and over 16 must register and use the application with the assistance of their parents or legal guardians, who are responsible for the acts performed by the minor within the platform.

Under Article 18 of the LGPD, users may request from NEO SOFT ENTERTAINMENT, at any time:
